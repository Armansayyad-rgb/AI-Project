The Ottoman Empire declined over a long period because of military defeats, territorial losses, economic problems, nationalist movements, and growing pressure from European powers. During the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, the empire lost large parts of its territory. The Ottoman Empire entered the First World War on the side of the Central Powers and was defeated. After the war, much of its remaining territory was occupied or divided, and the Ottoman sultanate was abolished in 1922. The Republic of Turkey was proclaimed in 1923, marking the end of the Ottoman Empire.
